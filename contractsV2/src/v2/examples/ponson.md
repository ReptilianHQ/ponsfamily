# Ponson ($PONSON)

### The official mascot of ponsfamily

![Ponson](./ponson.png)

> **"He already knows what you're about to say."**

Ponson is the official mascot of **ponsfamily**.

He is the character that represents the personality of the ecosystem: calm, skeptical, slightly unimpressed, and never in a hurry to believe what he's being told.

Soon, Ponson will also become an **interactive agent inside the ponsfamily website**, helping users understand the protocol, launches, and everything happening behind the interface.

For now, his first job is simple:

**explaining Pons V2.**

---

## 01 — Who Is Ponson?

Ponson is a cat.

He isn't designed to look heroic, aggressive, or overly friendly. He is deliberately calm.

His arms are crossed, his posture is relaxed, and his expression carries the feeling that he has already heard the pitch before you even finished making it.

He doesn't chase attention.

He doesn't need to.

That's the personality ponsfamily wanted its mascot to have: **confident without trying to prove anything.**

## 02 — The Look

Ponson's design is intentionally simple and recognizable.

The `P` is his only real accessory.

It isn't there to explain who he is.

# 05 — Ponson Explains Pons V2

The first thing Ponson is being used for is explaining **Pons V2**, the new launch architecture of ponsfamily.

The easiest way to understand it is to follow a token from its creation to its final market.

```text
Creation
   ↓
Bonding Curve
   ↓
Trading
   ↓
Graduation
   ↓
Uniswap V4
   ↓
Locked Liquidity
```

---

## 06 — Step 1: The Launch

Everything starts with a creator.

They choose the token's name, symbol, artwork, description and launch configuration.

Pons V2 checks the configuration and creates the token together with its bonding curve.

The launch is now live.

---

## 07 — Step 2: The Bonding Curve

Instead of starting with a normal liquidity pool, the token begins trading on a **bonding curve**.

The curve determines how the price changes as people buy and sell.

As more activity happens, the token moves closer to its graduation point.

This is the first stage of the token's life.

---

## 08 — Step 3: The Launch Window

The first moments of a launch are different.

Pons V2 uses an **anti-snipe mechanism** that makes the earliest seconds progressively less attractive to opportunistic buyers.

The protection gradually disappears as the launch develops.

The idea is simple:

> **Give the launch time to breathe before normal trading begins.**

---

## 09 — Step 4: Graduation

Eventually, the bonding curve reaches its graduation condition.

At that point, the token has completed its initial launch phase.

Pons V2 takes the reserves generated by the curve and prepares the token for its permanent market.

This is where the launch changes from:

**bonding curve → Uniswap V4**

---

## 10 — Step 5: The New Market

Pons V2 creates the token's **Uniswap V4 pool** using the final state of the bonding curve.

Liquidity is created as a full-range position and sent directly into the launch's locking mechanism.

The creator doesn't have to manually create the pool or handle the LP position.

The protocol does it.

---

## 11 — Step 6: Done

Once graduation is complete, the bonding curve has served its purpose.

The token now has its permanent market on Uniswap V4, with its liquidity locked through the protocol.

The complete journey looks like this:

```text

```
